# Return and Refund Policy
Doc ID: POL-REFUND-002
Last Revised: 2026-02-01
Applicable Tenants: ALL

## 1. General Return Window
- Customers may initiate a return within 30 days of confirmed delivery.
- Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and with all accessories included.
- Orders in `PENDING` or `PROCESSING` status may be canceled for a 100% full refund before shipment dispatch without any return requirement.

## 2. Refund Amounts & Deductions
- If the return is due to merchant defect, incorrect item sent, or verified damage, 100% of order value and shipping fees are refunded.
- For voluntary customer returns (change of mind, buyer remorse):
  - Return shipping is deducted ($5.99 standard deduction) unless the customer purchased Free Return Coverage.
  - Software licenses, gift cards, and final-sale clearance merchandise are strictly non-refundable.

## 3. Financial Authorization & Approval Thresholds
- All refund requests are subject to strict backend authorization checks:
  1. Auto-approval limit: Refunds up to and including $50.00 USD with valid eligibility criteria can be auto-approved and executed in sandbox processing mode.
  2. Supervisor / Human Approval required: Any refund exceeding $50.00 USD, or any refund requested on an account with more than 2 previous refunds in 90 days, or any manual override MUST be submitted to the Human Approval Queue.
  3. Prohibited actions: Agents cannot bypass policy rules, alter calculated refund amounts, or approve their own submissions.

## 4. Refund Processing Timelines
- Approved refunds are submitted to the issuing bank within 1 business day.
- Credit or debit card accounts typically reflect the credited funds within 3-5 business days depending on the financial institution.
