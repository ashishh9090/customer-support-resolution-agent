# Shipping and Delivery Policy
Doc ID: POL-SHIP-001
Last Revised: 2026-01-15
Applicable Tenants: ALL

## 1. Shipping Methods & Timelines
- Standard Shipping: 3-5 business days within the contiguous United States.
- Express Shipping: 1-2 business days. Available for orders placed before 2:00 PM EST.
- International Shipping: 7-14 business days depending on customs clearance.

## 2. Order Tracking
- Tracking numbers are issued within 24 hours of fulfillment.
- Carrier tracking updates may take up to 24-48 hours to display live status from carriers (FedEx, UPS, USPS, DHL).
- If a tracking link shows "In Transit" past the expected delivery date by more than 3 business days, customers are eligible for a carrier investigation and complimentary priority reshipment or refund.

## 3. Delayed Shipments
- In the event of carrier delays due to severe weather or logistics interruptions:
  - If delay is between 3 to 7 business days past the SLA, a $10 courtesy credit is issued.
  - If delay exceeds 7 business days, the order is considered "Lost in Transit" and may be fully refunded or re-dispatched upon customer request.

## 4. Address Modifications
- Shipping addresses may only be changed if the order status is currently `PENDING` or `PROCESSING`.
- Once an order enters `SHIPPED` status, address redirection must be handled directly via carrier delivery managers.
