# Damaged and Defective Product Policy
Doc ID: POL-DAMAGE-003
Last Revised: 2026-02-15
Applicable Tenants: ALL

## 1. Reporting Window
- Damaged, defective, or missing items must be reported within 14 calendar days of delivery.
- Immediate inspection upon receipt is strongly recommended.

## 2. Evidence & Verification Requirements
- Customers must provide:
  1. The Order ID and affected SKU or item name.
  2. A description of the damage or operational defect.
  3. Photographic evidence or serial number verification when requested.
- For minor cosmetic damage under $40, a photo waiver or instant partial refund (20-30%) may be offered.

## 3. Resolution Workflows
- **Option A - Expedited Replacement**: A replacement unit is dispatched within 24 hours at zero additional cost with complimentary return shipping label for the defective unit.
- **Option B - Full Refund**: If a replacement is out of stock or declined by the customer, a full refund is scheduled. If value exceeds $50.00, approval is routed to human operations.
- Hazardous or broken glass items must NOT be shipped back; customers should safely dispose of them once approved.
