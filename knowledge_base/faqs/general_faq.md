# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Doc ID: FAQ-GEN-004
Last Revised: 2026-03-01
Applicable Tenants: ALL

## Q1: How do I track my order?
You can check real-time order status by providing your Order ID (e.g., `ORD-1001`) and registered email address to our support assistant or visiting the My Account > Orders tab. Once your package ships, your carrier tracking number (FedEx, UPS, USPS) will be visible.

## Q2: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
Address changes are permitted only while an order is in `PENDING` or `PROCESSING` status. Once an order is marked `SHIPPED`, carriers handle parcel routing and changes must be requested through carrier delivery management tools.

## Q3: What payment methods are supported?
We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and verified bank transfers.

## Q4: How do I speak with a human support manager?
You can request human agent escalation at any time. Our system automatically routes complex disputes, high-value transactions, or ambiguous claims to the Human Approval and Escalation Center. A human support specialist typically responds within 15-30 minutes during business hours.

## Q5: How do warranties work on electronics and hardware?
All hardware products come with a minimum 1-year manufacturer warranty covering operational defects. Extended warranties (up to 3 years) cover accidental damage when purchased at checkout.
